# How does Min-Max scaling work and what is the resulting range of values?

## Answer

Min-Max scaling, also known as normalization, is a crucial step in data preprocessing that transforms numerical features to a common scale without distorting differences in the ranges of values or losing information about relative magnitudes. The goal is to ensure that all features contribute equally to the analysis and are on a similar scale.
