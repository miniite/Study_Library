# What is your framework for handling a data ownership dispute between two senior directors who both claim authority over the customer 360 dataset?

**Difficulty:** Deep · **Levels:** Architect/Principal, SDE-2/3 · [↑ Canonical Model vs Domain Autonomy](<../../../../05_Data_Governance_and_Security/Governance/Stewardship/Canonical_Model_vs_Domain_Autonomy.md>)

> ⚠️ Practice answer, not verified. Some answers contain mistakes; when in doubt, follow the lesson.

## Answer

When faced with a data ownership dispute between two senior directors, it's crucial to establish a structured and comprehensive approach to resolve the issue effectively. This scenario involves not only resolving the immediate conflict but also ensuring that data governance practices are robust enough to prevent such disputes in the future.

First, I would begin by gathering detailed information about both claims and the context surrounding the dispute. Then, I'd evaluate the organizational structure and existing policies related to data ownership. Finally, I’d propose a framework that addresses not just this immediate conflict but also long-term governance practices.

**Comparison:**

```
Step	Action
1.0	Gather detailed information about both directors' claims and the context of the dispute.
2.0	Evaluate current data governance policies, processes, and practices.
3.0	Identify key stakeholders involved in the dataset management and ensure their involvement throughout the process.
4.0	Propose a resolution framework that includes roles, responsibilities, and communication protocols.
5.0	Implement the proposed framework with clear policies and workflows.
6.0	Monitor the implementation and address any issues promptly.
7.0	Periodically review and iterate the governance practices based on feedback and changing organizational needs.
```
