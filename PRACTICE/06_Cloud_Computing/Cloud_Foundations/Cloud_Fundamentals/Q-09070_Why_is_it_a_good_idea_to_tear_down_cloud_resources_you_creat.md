# Why is it a good idea to tear down cloud resources you created for a college project when you are done?

**Difficulty:** Basic · **Levels:** Architect/Principal, Fresher, SDE-1/2, SDE-2/3 · [↑ Control Plane Failures](<../../../../06_Cloud_Computing/Cloud_Foundations/Cloud_Fundamentals/Safe_Config_Rollouts/Control_Plane_Failures.md>)

> ⚠️ Practice answer, not verified. Some answers contain mistakes; when in doubt, follow the lesson.

## Answer

When you complete your college projects involving cloud services, tearing down the resources you've provisioned can save costs and avoid unnecessary usage. This practice is a fundamental aspect of cloud management that helps maintain financial discipline and resource efficiency. In this explanation, we will delve into why it's a good idea to tear down these resources from both practical and strategic perspectives.
